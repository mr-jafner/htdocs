Why you, specifically

01

The regulatory floor is shifting under the industry

Fealty arrives as the FAA moves from waiver-by-waiver BVLOS to a standards-based Part 108 framework. The disciplines Part 108 leans on — consensus airworthiness, performance-based safety cases, recordkeeping, SMS — are functionally cousins of the AIAG-VDA / AS9145 / DFMEA stack I run in my day job.

02

You've tied the manufacturing thesis to Detroit's auto supply chain

That's not a brand line for me — it's the ecosystem I've spent fifteen years inside. I know what Tier-1 PPAP looks like. I know what supplier quality drift looks like at month nine of a launch. I know how to hold a control plan together when nobody wants to look at it.

03

You're at the headcount where everyone still does everything

Which is great until it isn't. The transition from "we know what's in each other's heads" to "we have systems that survive someone leaving" is hard, and most engineering managers I respect have done it at least once. I have.
